Matching rules implemented

  1. Returning tutor continuity is processed first. If a tutor listed a returning tutee and at least one availability slot overlaps, the tutor is assigned to that tutee first.
  2. Availability is a hard rule for every assigned tutor.
  3. Each tutee session must include at least one tutor who speaks the tutee's native language, unless the session is flagged for manual review.
  4. New tutees are prioritized before returning tutees after continuity matches.
  5. The number of tutors needed depends on naturalization stage, returning status, and requested weekly sessions.
  6. In-person or Zoom preference is optimized after hard rules, and lower-ranked tutors may have their preference overridden when needed.
