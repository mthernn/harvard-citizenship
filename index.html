Seniority rules used

  1. Director, Secretary, current Board Member
  2. Former Director, former Secretary, former Board Member
  3. Years since selected for former highest position, largest to smallest
  4. Returning Member
  5. Class Year, smallest to largest
  6. Number of languages other than English, largest to smallest
  7. Specific language priority from Spanish through Latin
  8. Birthdate, oldest to youngest
  9. Surname, then Forename, A to Z
